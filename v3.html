ORGANIZE / QUESTION / KEEP

Your sources are the beginning.
Your understanding is the point.

Follow the workflow inside TubeUnpack.
Every screen below is the actual application.

01 / ORGANIZE

Your channels.
By topic.
One click away.

Bring your subscriptions together by topic—finance, technology, science, or whatever matters to you. Once organized, open a category to see its channels and their latest videos together, without searching through your subscriptions again.

Import all subscriptions or choose individual channels. Use suggested categories or make your own.

WATCH INSIDE TUBEUNPACK

The video.
The transcript.
The same workspace.

Watch in the embedded player, read alongside it and use available transcript timestamps to return to a passage. Keep your attention on the material, not on switching windows.

Captions depend on availability. A transcript records a claim; it does not verify it. Some videos must be watched on YouTube.

02 / QUESTION

Ask for an examination.
Not just a summary.

Prepare the transcript and a detailed prompt in a few clicks. The deep prompt asks your assistant to reconstruct the argument, examine its support, identify weaknesses and flag claims that need independent verification.

  1. Copy Deep + Transcript
  2. Open your assistant, review, paste and submit.
  3. Check important claims against independent sources.

AI output is a starting point, not proof. Opening an assistant does not automatically send your material.

03 / KEEP

Return to the research.
Not to square one.

Copy back useful analysis. Keep the research question, your conclusions, verification sources and original video references together in a named workspace.

Reopen it when the topic comes up again. Search saved transcripts, notes and results, or export the research as Markdown.

ROOM TO FOLLOW THE QUESTION

Look wider. Make it yours.

Find more evidence.
Look for a counterargument.

Search wider YouTube, explore countries and categories, and browse a channel’s older uploads. Use discovery to test an idea—not only to find more of the same.

Regional trends are discovery signals, not a measure of truth or importance. Channel history depends on what has been loaded and indexed.

A quick first look.
A deeper next step.

Quick Brief extracts an initial overview from available text or metadata. When a claim deserves more attention, move it to the Analysis Desk and choose a simple or deep prompt.

Quick Brief is an extractive overview, not an AI fact-check. ChatGPT is the default assistant; Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Grok are also available choices.

Your organization.
Your working rhythm.

Save video references into local folders, organize several videos together, use separate tabs for different tasks and choose a light or dark theme that suits you.

Local folders do not download video files or automatically change YouTube playlists.

Keep a separate copy.

Export your workspace locally or choose optional Google Drive backups. Return to your research with the sources, questions and useful results together.

Drive backups are optional, not continuous device sync. Backup encryption does not encrypt the app’s working files.

WRITTEN INSTRUCTIONS

From first launch to your first investigation.

Save these instructions (.md)

The Windows installer is being prepared. Invited testers should use the build and Google connection instructions supplied with their invitation.

  1. Install and open TubeUnpack

    Open the supplied Windows installer and follow its instructions. Launch TubeUnpack to open the dashboard in your browser. The welcome guide appears on a fresh profile.

  2. Connect YouTube and choose your channels

    Choose Start setup, then Connect YouTube. Sign in on Google’s page with the account containing your subscriptions and review the requested permissions. Import all subscriptions or select the channels you want, then review their categories.

    The connected beta requests viewing and management access to YouTube. Local organization is separate from account actions such as playlist changes or unsubscribing.

  3. Find, watch and inspect

    Refresh your feed and narrow it by category, channel, personal priority or search. Save useful filter combinations. Play a video inside TubeUnpack, read its available transcript and use timestamp links to revisit a passage. Quick Brief gives an initial overview extracted from available text or metadata.

  4. Prepare and run the analysis

    Choose Send to Analysis Desk, then Copy Deep + Transcript. Open your preferred assistant, paste and review the prepared text, and submit it. ChatGPT is the default; you can choose another assistant in Setup & Preferences. Follow up on the response and verify important claims against independent sources.

  5. Keep the useful results

    Save video references into folders. Copy useful AI output into Save analysis result, add notes and verification sources, and keep the investigation in Saved research. Reopen it, search previous work or export it as Markdown.

  6. Make it your workspace

    Choose a light or dark theme, adjust the player layout and use separate tabs for different tasks. In Backup & restore, choose whether to export a local backup or connect your own Google Drive.

Windows security notices

The current candidate is unsigned. Use only the build supplied with your invitation and verify the version and source if Windows shows a warning. Do not disable Windows protection.

Google sign-in and permissions

While Google authorization is in testing, your Google account must be eligible for the private beta. Review the TubeUnpack application name and requested access on Google’s own page. If the app or account is unfamiliar, stop and check with the organizer.

Google may display permission checkboxes or an existing-access summary. Review the access required for the connected workflow before choosing Continue. Return to TubeUnpack and confirm that the expected account is connected.

The existing walkthrough shows an earlier Source Watch connection flow. Google’s screens can differ according to your account and previous permissions.

PRIVACY, WITHOUT THE SMALL PRINT

Your working library stays with you.

Stored on your computer

Sources, video references, notes, cached transcripts and saved research live on your computer rather than in a TubeUnpack cloud account. Local storage does not mean the entire workspace is encrypted.

Connected when you choose

Google and YouTube provide sign-in, account services, video data and embedded playback. You choose what text to submit to your AI assistant; that service processes it under its own settings and policies.

Your choice of backup

Keep a local export or separately connect your Google Drive. Uploads happen when you request them or enable automatic backups. Backup-file encryption is on by default; keep your password safe. Working files and restore safety copies remain unencrypted.

A folder synced by OneDrive or another service may also be copied to that service. This website has no added analytics or account system. Playing the guide connects to YouTube; hosting and external services may process connection information.
