HOW TO COMPARE

Three useful questions.

  1. Can a new visitor explain what TubeUnpack does after the first screen?
  2. Do the demonstrations make the next action clear, especially saving and finding research?
  3. Can an invited tester find the installer, setup instructions and feedback checklist without searching?
