A FIRST USEFUL SESSION

One source.
A question worth keeping.

You do not need to learn every feature first. Follow a single video from your feed to a saved result.

  1. Install, open and connect.

    Open the installer and follow its instructions, then launch TubeUnpack from Start. Follow Start setup → Connect YouTube using your invited Google account.

    Review Google’s viewing and management permissions before connecting.

    Installation and first-run guide ↗

  2. Pick one video. Ask one question.

    Choose your channels, open a video and read its available transcript. What claim do you want to understand?

  3. Copy, paste, investigate.

    Send the video to Analysis Desk. Choose Copy Deep + Transcript, open your preferred AI assistant, review the pasted text and submit it yourself.

    Nothing is submitted or retrieved automatically. Check important findings independently.

    Prepare an analysis and choose an assistant ↗

  4. Save the useful part. Reopen it.

    Manually paste useful output into Save analysis result. Find it later in Saved Research → Saved Analyses, with your notes and source reference.

BEFORE YOU CONNECT

Local research.
External services by choice.

Your sources, notes and saved findings stay on this computer by default. Google services, YouTube playback, text you send to an AI assistant and optional Drive backups use external services.

Backup encryption does not encrypt working research files or restore safety copies.
