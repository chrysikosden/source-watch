01 / ORGANIZE

Your channels.
A feed that makes sense.

Your topics—not a crowded subscription list.

  1. Import all or selected channels.
  2. Assign suggested categories or your own.
  3. Open a topic’s videos in Latest Long-form.

02 / WATCH

Watch the video.
Stay close to the source.

Keep the video and the words behind it in the same workspace.

  1. Open a video inside TubeUnpack.
  2. Read its available transcript alongside.
  3. Click a timestamp to revisit the passage.

Captions depend on availability. Some videos must be watched on YouTube.

03 / ANALYSIS DESK

Investigate the argument,
claim by claim.

Turn the transcript into a focused investigation of the argument, evidence and missing context.

Read the analysis guide ↗
  1. Copy a Simple or Deep prompt + transcript.
  2. Paste, review and submit to your preferred AI assistant.
  3. Follow up and check important claims independently.

TubeUnpack prepares the material; you submit it and paste back anything you want to save. Opening an assistant sends nothing automatically. AI output is a starting point, not proof.

04 / SAVED RESEARCH

More perspectives.
One continuing question.

Return to your sources and your thinking together.

Read the research guide ↗
  1. Search Wider YouTube for other perspectives.
  2. Add relevant videos to a research project.
  3. Paste useful findings, add notes and save.

Saved Research separates Research Projects and Saved Analyses. One editable response is retained per video; export before replacing it.

All four demonstrations use actual application captures and real public video material, filmed without account details or private research. Video creators are not affiliated with TubeUnpack.

ROOM TO FOLLOW THE QUESTION

A working library,
not another watchlist.

Compare sources

Prepare complete prompts and transcripts from several selected videos. Copy or export the package, then submit it to your AI assistant yourself.

Missing transcripts and provider length limits still matter.

Three ways to keep your work

Saved & Folders: video references for later.
Research Projects: videos and notes around a question.
Saved Analyses: responses you paste back.

Organize your way

Use saved filters, separate tabs and light or dark themes to fit your working rhythm.

Saved folders keep references, not downloaded video files.

Choose your backup

Export a local backup or connect optional Google Drive backups. Keep a separate copy of your working library.

Drive backup is not continuous device sync.

YOUR WORK, YOUR CHOICES

Local research.
Clear boundaries.

Read the privacy guide ↗

On your computer

Your sources, references, transcripts, notes and saved findings are stored locally—not in a TubeUnpack cloud account.

Connected by choice

Google and YouTube provide account services, video data and playback. You choose what to submit to an AI assistant; that provider’s policies apply.

Backup is separate

Google Drive backup is optional. Backup encryption does not encrypt working research files or restore safety copies. A synced data folder may also be copied by its sync service.
