WHAT YOU KEEP

A named result for a video.

Paste a response from your assistant or write your own analysis in Save analysis result. The name, text and verification notes stay linked to the source video.

WHERE YOU RETURN

Saved Research → Saved Analyses.

Open the named result to read, edit or export it. Its saved text remains available even if the video leaves Analysis Desk or its research project is deleted.

KEEP ANOTHER VERSION?

Export before replacing.

TubeUnpack stores one editable response per video. Export it before saving over it when you want to retain a separate version.
