Open a source without losing the workspace

Open a video from the feed, saved library or another video view. You can also paste its YouTube URL into the top address bar to open a separate video workbench without first adding it to the Analysis Desk.

The official YouTube player appears inside TubeUnpack. Description and Transcript are separate information tabs beside playback. Description is creator-provided context, not independent evidence that a claim is true.

Playback contacts YouTube and requires online access. TubeUnpack does not promise offline video playback. If a restricted video will not play inside the workspace, use Open in YouTube.

Return to the actual passage

When a transcript includes usable timestamps, its timestamp buttons seek inside the embedded player. Follow playback keeps the matching text in view while available synchronized timestamps allow it.

Manual transcript scrolling can pause following so the page does not fight your reading. Choose Resume following to return to it; a timeline seek also resumes following. You can turn following off.

Text without synchronized timestamps can still be read or copied, but it cannot reliably follow the current playback position. Do not infer precise timing from untimed pasted text.

When captions are unavailable

Caption availability varies. TubeUnpack may show No transcript yet, Loading transcript, Transcript ready or Transcript unavailable. A failure is not proof that the video has no words—it may mean the caption path cannot retrieve them.

  1. Try a different available language

    Choose caption language lets you inspect another caption option. The language you choose still needs to be available.

  2. Provide text deliberately

    Paste transcript lets you save a transcript with an optional language label. Use material you are entitled to use, check its coverage and note where it came from.

  3. Keep the limit visible

    If you cannot obtain a usable transcript, inspect the description and video directly. Do not treat a metadata-only brief as a transcript analysis or invent missing quotations.

A complete copy of stored text is not a guarantee of accurate captions or complete coverage of the original video. Generated captions may contain errors; check the original before relying on a quote.

Quick Brief is a first look, not AI

Quick Brief uses local rules to extract key sentences. Its label distinguishes a transcript-based brief from one using only the creator's title/description.

Useful for orientation

Use it to decide whether to read further or prepare an analysis package. It helps you skim the available material.

Not a verdict

It is not an LLM summary, an independent fact-check or a guarantee that the central argument was captured. It does not establish external truth.

Make the reading space comfortable

Resize the video window on desktop, drag the divider, use Expand / Restore or choose Theater layout. In Setup & Preferences → Video player & tabs, set:

  • Click outside to close, Press Escape to close and Remember window size.
  • Open video links in TubeUnpack or YouTube.
  • Player layout: Side by side or Theater.
  • Information panel: Description or Transcript.
  • Follow timestamped transcript and your Preferred assistant website.

By default, videos stay inside TubeUnpack and the window requires X or Escape to close rather than an outside click.

Middle-click or Ctrl-click supported navigation/video links to open independent browser tabs. Local saves and feed results synchronize; navigation and selections stay per tab. Save any draft before switching context, and read conflict warnings instead of assuming one tab's edit can safely overwrite another.

Reading is the start of the research

Save the video to a local folder, add it to a Research Project or send it to the Analysis Desk. The compact analysis actions prepare prompts and transcripts, then open your chosen assistant website.

Nothing is sent to an assistant automatically. You copy, paste, submit and later paste a reviewed response back into TubeUnpack.
