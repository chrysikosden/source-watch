Guide 05 / Investigate deliberately

From a video
to a better question.

TubeUnpack prepares the material. Your chosen assistant does the AI work. You decide what to send, what to trust and what to keep.

You stay in the loop

  1. 01PrepareInspect the source and load or paste its transcript.
  2. 02CopyChoose the prompt and complete transcript package.
  3. 03PasteOpen your assistant and submit the material yourself.
  4. 04KeepReview the response and paste it back into TubeUnpack.

Opening an assistant does not transfer your text or start analysis. TubeUnpack does not submit transcripts automatically or retrieve your AI conversations.

Start with one video

  1. Open a source

    Open a video from the feed or paste its URL into the top address bar. The video workbench has playback, description, transcript and analysis controls. Use it directly or send the video to the Analysis Desk.

  2. Check the transcript

    Load available captions. The initiating copy control shows fetching, preparing, copying and completion—or failure. You can choose another caption language or paste a transcript when retrieval is unavailable.

  3. Choose the depth

    Copy Simple + Transcript combines the original summary instructions with the complete stored transcript. Copy Deep + Transcript combines the critical-analysis instructions with it. Prompt-only and transcript-only actions are also available.

    Setup & Preferences → Analysis buttons controls which actions you see. Restore defaults brings them back.

  4. Send and save manually

    Open your preferred assistant, choose its model and paste. When you have reviewed its response, return to Save analysis result, paste the text, name it and optionally add its video to a project. The successful save offers View saved result.

Find and manage the result in Saved Analyses →

Your assistant, your choice

The arrow beside Open assistant changes one shared preferred website. The same choice appears in Setup & Preferences.

  • ChatGPT
  • Gemini
  • Claude
  • Perplexity
  • Grok
  • DeepSeek

These are website launch options, not built-in API integrations or included AI subscriptions. Select the provider's model after opening its website. Its input limits, terms and handling of text apply to material you send.

The Gemini launcher is not a Gemini transcription integration. TubeUnpack does not add a new transcription service in this candidate.

Bring sources into the same conversation

Select videos independently on the Analysis Desk. Load missing transcripts, choose Simple summary or Deep analysis, then copy the comparison package or download its text file.

What goes into the package

A source index with titles, channels and links; the complete per-video prompts and stored transcripts; available language/origin information; and comparison instructions that ask for attribution, agreements, disagreements, evidence and uncertainty.

Inside a Research Project, select the sources above its videos. Copy transcripts alone, copy with a comparison prompt, or use Copy + open to copy and open your preferred assistant. You still paste and submit it yourself.

Nothing silently omitted

Desk copying is blocked until every selected transcript is ready. Research Projects offer retry or an explicit choice to continue with only the available sources.

Nothing silently shortened

Included text is not truncated to fit a model. A large-package warning gives you the choice to reduce the selection, copy the full text or download a text file.

Length matters more than video count

Two long interviews can contain more text than twenty short videos. TubeUnpack shows characters and a rough, language/model-dependent token estimate. Its 150,000-character caution is a UI warning—not a verified limit for any assistant.

A model may reject a large paste or file. Even when it accepts the material, it may miss important details. Leave room for the response, use a fresh conversation and check its source references.

Captions are not guaranteed

Captions may be missing, inaccessible, generated imperfectly or in a different language. A complete copy of the stored transcript is not proof that the transcript perfectly represents the whole video. Retry, select another language or provide your own text, and note the limitation.

For large groups, analyze sources separately before comparing their saved responses. That is a useful alternative, but it is not the same as comparing every full transcript. A downloaded file does not guarantee the provider can process it.

Use the response as working material

Compare source claims with evidence. Check quotations and timestamps against the original video. Treat agreement between sources as a lead, not proof—several sources may repeat the same origin.

Your channel priority is a personal preference, not a credibility score. TubeUnpack's Discovery ranking uses metadata heuristics; it does not certify truth or objectivity. Quick Brief extracts sentences using rules and labels whether it used a transcript or only creator metadata. It is not AI analysis.
