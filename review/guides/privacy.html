Guide 07 / Know the boundaries

Local-first.
Not disconnected.

Your research stays on this computer by default. Playback, Google services, optional Drive backups and text you send to an assistant cross different boundaries.

Clear choices · Separate permissions · No blanket privacy promises

On this page

What “local-first” means here

TubeUnpack runs a local server and opens its dashboard in your browser. Sources, saves, notes, cached transcripts, research and local profiles are stored on your computer—not in a TubeUnpack cloud account.

Default Windows data location

%LOCALAPPDATA%\SourceWatchV4

Program files and user data are separate. Keep this data folder private; do not send the whole folder with a bug report.

Switching Google accounts switches the active local profile. Windows protects the OAuth token for your Windows user. That does not make the entire workspace encrypted or protect against everything running as you.

Working files are not promised encrypted

Local research files and pre-restore safety copies may contain readable private material. Encryption of an exported backup does not encrypt the app's working files.

What uses an external service?

Local storage is different from network use
ActionBoundary
Play or seek a videoThe official embedded player contacts YouTube. This is not offline playback.
Connect, import, search or refreshGoogle/YouTube receive the relevant service requests. Wider search and history indexing use normal service quota.
Open an assistantThe browser opens that provider's website. TubeUnpack does not transfer your package automatically.
Paste material into an assistantYou deliberately send text to the provider. Its handling and terms apply.
Use a local backupYou choose where the exported file goes; keep it private.
Connect and use Google Drive backupA separate Drive connection and requested/opted-in upload place a backup in Drive.

Local saved folders do not alter YouTube playlists. Supported playlist/subscription changes require verified access and deliberate actions. The normal Google connection includes management permission, not just read access.

Choose a backup you can recover

Export locally

Save a backup file to a location you control. Encryption is on by default. Use a strong, unique password of at least 12 characters and keep it in a password manager.

Connect Drive separately

Drive backup uses its own connection. Connecting does not upload a backup. Upload when requested, or deliberately opt in to automatic backups.

Automatic Drive backups are off by default. When enabled, they can run once a day while the app is open. Encrypted automatic backups require unlocking in the tab; the password stays in browser memory and is cleared on reload. It is not saved in browser storage or uploaded.

Keep the password. Without it, an encrypted backup cannot be recovered. Optional plaintext exports/uploads require an explicit warning because anyone with the file can read its included private material.

Backup inclusion choices matter: select the workspace/setup mode and whether cached transcripts/feed belong in the export. Selected browser preferences can transfer. Personal authorization credentials are not intended as portable workspace content.

Preview before replacing

Read the restore preview before applying it. Choose replace or merge deliberately and review conflict/preference choices. TubeUnpack creates a pre-restore safety copy of the current workspace.

That safety copy is useful for recovery, but it is not promised encrypted. Keep the data folder and your backup destination private. Cancel when the contents or destination are not what you expected.

The candidate has packaged checks for encrypted/plain exports, wrong passwords, preview/cancel, merge/replace and safety copies. Drive and native Save As were mocked in those tests; live Drive acceptance remains separate.

Disconnecting is not erasing

Disconnect removes the local active Google authorization without silently deleting imported sources or saved research. If you also want to revoke Google-side access, do that separately in your Google account.

Uninstall retains research by default. Interactive per-user uninstall offers two explicit confirmations before local data deletion; silent/all-users uninstall keeps data. Uninstall alone cannot revoke Google-side access.

Report a problem, not your private workspace

Useful feedback includes the app version, Windows/browser versions, what you clicked, what you expected and the visible error. Use a small non-sensitive example.

Do not send OAuth tokens, configuration files, passwords, account details, whole data folders, private transcripts or unredacted logs/screenshots. Check the report before sharing it with the person who invited you.
