WATCH & EXAMINE

Keep the video
beside the evidence.

Play videos inside TubeUnpack with the description or transcript alongside. Click an available timestamp to revisit a passage, or let the transcript follow playback while you listen.

  • Resize the player and adjust the side panel.
  • Switch between side-by-side and theater layouts.
  • Open on YouTube whenever you prefer.

Captions and timestamp navigation depend on what is available for each video. Some videos must be watched on YouTube.

Bring your preferred assistant

Prepare a transcript and a simple or deep analysis prompt, then open ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity or Grok. You review, paste and submit the material yourself.

Uses your account with that service. Opening it does not send your research or run an analysis.

Build a library that stays useful

Save references into one or more folders, organize selections together and drag videos between folders. Search saved transcripts, notes and results to find an argument again.

Local folders keep video references and research; they do not download video files or automatically change YouTube playlists.

Explore another perspective

Search for more evidence or a counterargument. Explore countries and categories, preview channels and browse older uploads when you want to go deeper.

Regional results are discovery signals. Channel history depends on what you have loaded and indexed.

Keep a copy of your work

Export and restore your setup or research workspace. Password-encrypted backup files and an optional connection to your Google Drive help you keep a separate copy.

Drive backups are optional. They are not continuous device sync. Backup encryption does not encrypt the app’s working files.

WRITTEN INSTRUCTIONS

From first launch to your first investigation.

Save these instructions (.md)

The Windows installer is being prepared. Invited testers should use the build and Google connection instructions provided with their invitation.

  1. Install and open TubeUnpack

    Open the supplied Windows installer and follow its instructions. TubeUnpack opens its dashboard in your browser. On a fresh profile, the welcome guide opens automatically.

    The existing video shows an earlier portable preview; the upcoming download uses a Windows installer. Use only the file supplied for your beta invitation. If Windows warns about the file, check the version and publisher information with the organizer; do not disable Windows protection.

  2. Connect YouTube first

    Choose Start setup, then Connect YouTube. This lets TubeUnpack load the subscriptions and playlists belonging to the account you want to use. Sign in on Google's own page, not inside a TubeUnpack password form.

    Choose the correct Google account and, if offered, the personal or brand identity containing your subscriptions.

    Google's warning, permissions and Continue buttons

    A private test build may display an unverified-app notice. Continue only if you are an invited tester, the app matches the expected TubeUnpack build, and you understand the requested access. If it is unfamiliar, choose the safe exit and ask the organizer.

    Review the application name and every permission. The current workflow requests viewing and management access to YouTube, as well as account identity information. It is not a read-only connection. Management access enables supported account actions such as playlist updates or unsubscribing when you explicitly request them.

    On a first connection, Google may show permission checkboxes: review and select the permissions required for the workflow before choosing Continue. A previously connected account may instead show an existing-access summary. Open the service details, review them, choose Done if shown, then Continue.

    Return to TubeUnpack, verify the connected account, and wait for subscriptions to load. The video records an existing-access summary; it does not recreate first-time checkboxes.

  3. Choose which subscriptions become sources

    Import the channels you want to monitor. Keep suggested categories, adjust them, or create your own. Choose Import all subscriptions or select individual channels. Assign categories and confirm the import.

  4. Refresh and inspect your feed

    Refresh to retrieve recent uploads. Browse Latest Long-form or Shorts and narrow by category, channel, status or search. Open Quick Brief for an extractive first inspection. Check whether it is based on a transcript or just limited metadata; it is not an AI truth verdict.

  5. Prepare the transcript and deep-analysis prompt

    Choose Send to Analysis Desk, then Copy Deep + Transcript. Review the source material and structured prompt. It helps you examine credibility, evidence, weak arguments and claims needing independent verification. Caption-language selection and manual transcript entry are available when needed.

    Watch inside the embedded player and use available transcript timestamps to revisit a passage. Click Open ChatGPT, or choose another assistant in Setup & Preferences. If you are signed in there in that browser, your signed-in account opens. Otherwise sign in on ChatGPT. Paste the packet, review it, and send it. Ask follow-up questions and check cited evidence yourself. An AI answer does not guarantee a claim is true.

  6. Save, select and organize

    Use Save to retain a video locally. Select multiple videos using their individual checkboxes, watch the selected count, and open Selected Videos. Choose a folder or batch action. Local saves and folders are separate from YouTube playlist changes; check the destination before confirming a YouTube action.

  7. Keep the investigation, not only the videos

    Copy useful ChatGPT output back into Save analysis result manually. Add verification sources and dates. Save a named workspace in Saved research, reopen it later, or export it as Markdown. Search My Research helps retrieve transcripts, notes and results. TubeUnpack does not automatically retrieve ChatGPT conversations.

  8. Go beyond your existing sources

    In Discovery, choose More evidence, Challenge this or Better source, and select the search scope. In Trending, choose a country and YouTube category. Explore by Country adds topics, language, recency and depth. These results are useful signals, not a representative survey of what a country's population considers important.

    In Channel Videos, choose a monitored channel, scroll through indexed videos, and use Search channel videos. Check archive coverage: a partial local index is not the channel's complete history.

  9. Make the workspace comfortable

    Choose a light or dark theme in the appearance settings. Your research remains in place. Use Help for reminders of the available pages and actions.

PRIVACY, WITHOUT THE SMALL PRINT

Your working library stays with you.

Stored on your computer

Sources, video references, notes, cached transcripts and saved research live on your computer rather than in a TubeUnpack cloud account. Local storage does not mean the entire workspace is encrypted.

Connected when you choose

Google and YouTube provide sign-in, account services, video data and embedded playback. You choose what text to submit to your AI assistant; that service processes it under its own settings and policies.

Your choice of backup

Keep a local export or separately connect your Google Drive. Uploads happen when you request them or enable automatic backups. Backup-file encryption is on by default; keep your password safe. Working files and restore safety copies remain unencrypted.

A folder synced by OneDrive or another service may also be copied to that service. This website has no added analytics or account system. Playing the guide connects to YouTube; hosting and external services may process connection information.
